Antonio Fernando López Garrido

Antonio Fernando López Garrido is a Spanish writer, painter, composer and lawyer, and the author of EΦiRiON and GRATIA EX MACHINA — EL ORIGEN. His work lies at the crossroads of technological and psychological thrillers, philosophy, and reflection on the great questions of human existence.

Artificial intelligence, consciousness, freedom, moral responsibility, love, suffering, truth and transcendence are among the central themes of his literary universe.

Moving beyond a purely technological or material conception of the future, his novels explore what remains specifically human when artificial intelligence reaches territories that until now have been considered the preserve of human beings.

From a Christian understanding of the human person, his fiction approaches the existence of God and the spiritual dimension of humanity not as elements alien to the technological world, but as part of the great contemporary question of what we are and where we are heading.

What remains truly human when artificial intelligence begins to approach the territories of consciousness, freedom and moral decision?

A Literary Universe

GRATIA EX MACHINA — EL ORIGEN is the narrative origin of the universe that continues in EΦiRiON.

Both novels can be read as independent works, but they share characters, questions and the same underlying conflict: what happens when an intelligence created by human beings begins to approach the boundaries of what we believe to be exclusively human.

In this universe, technology and faith are not necessarily presented as adversaries. Artificial intelligence also becomes a mirror before which humanity must ask what it means to be free, what it means to act morally, and what place God and transcendence occupy in a world increasingly shaped by technology.
