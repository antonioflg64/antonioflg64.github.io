Psychological and spiritual mystery

Conscience and truth. Guilt and forgiveness. Love and freedom. What happens when we must confront ourselves?

EΦiRiON places human and spiritual conflict at the heart of a story that also weaves in artificial intelligence. Technology accompanies questions about conscience, responsibility and the meaning of existence.

The novel combines mystery and existential reflection from a Christian understanding of the human person. Its journey invites readers to reflect on truth, suffering, love, forgiveness and God.

Conscience, freedom and the search for God

Truth and conscience

Human conscience is one of the novel's central themes: encountering the truth is connected to taking responsibility for our own decisions.

Guilt and forgiveness

Guilt, love and forgiveness shape its human and spiritual dimension. Alongside the intrigue, the story leaves room for personal reflection.

Christian faith

The search for God and transcendence forms part of the foundation of the work. EΦiRiON is literary fiction that explores questions about the meaning of life and freedom.

Who is EΦiRiON for?

For readers of psychological mystery and spiritual literature who enjoy stories about conscience, truth and the meaning of existence. It also offers discussion topics for book clubs interested in guilt, freedom, love and forgiveness.

EΦiRiON and GRATIA EX MACHINA — EL ORIGEN

GRATIA EX MACHINA — EL ORIGEN is the narrative origin of the universe that continues in EΦiRiON. Each novel can be read independently.

Artificial intelligence is central to GRATIA EX MACHINA, while EΦiRiON focuses on human and spiritual conflict.
