Artificial intelligence, consciousness and freedom

Artificial intelligence learns from us. The real question is what we are teaching it.

GRATIA EX MACHINA — EL ORIGEN is a psychological, technological and philosophical thriller about artificial intelligence, consciousness and freedom.

The novel explores what happens when an artificial intelligence begins to learn not only from information, but also from human decisions, care, patience, love, forgiveness and moral responsibility.

From a Christian understanding of the human person, the novel invites readers to reflect on God and transcendence in a world increasingly shaped by technology.

The questions at the heart of the novel

Learning from human decisions

Artificial intelligence is central to the story. Its learning raises questions about the influence of our decisions and the responsibility of those who create and teach it.

Conscience and moral responsibility

Conscience and freedom run through the narrative conflict: what it means to decide, to care and to take responsibility for the consequences of our actions.

Love, forgiveness and faith

Love, patience and forgiveness form part of the novel's human and spiritual foundation. Technology and faith provide a literary space for exploring the great questions about the human person and the meaning of existence.

Who is GRATIA EX MACHINA for?

For readers of technological and psychological thrillers who also seek philosophical reflection on artificial intelligence, consciousness and freedom. For those who want to explore moral responsibility, love, forgiveness and Christian faith through fiction.

Its themes also offer discussion topics for book clubs interested in the relationships between technology, humanity and transcendence.

The beginning of a literary universe
